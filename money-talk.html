🎤 Speaking task · Money & Saving

📋 What to do

  1. Read the six topics below.
  2. Click the topic you like best — it turns green.
  3. Use the points and the tip to prepare a short talk (about 1–2 minutes).
  4. Speak freely — you may use a few key words, but don't read everything!

🧒

Money for teens

  • Where teens get money: pocket money, gifts, a small job
  • How much pocket money do you get? Is it enough?
  • What do teens usually spend their money on?
  • Why it is important to handle money well
  • Money you learn to manage now helps you as an adult
  • Talk about your own example: what do you do with your money?
Tip: don't spend everything at once.
2
🤔

Needs or wants

  • A need is something you must have (food, clothes, school things)
  • A want is something nice but not necessary (games, sweets, new shoes)
  • Some things can be both — explain when
  • Before you buy, ask: "Do I really need this?"
  • Needs come first, wants come after
  • Give your own examples of a need and a want
  • Advertising often makes wants look like needs
Tip: wait before buying a want.
3
🏦

Saving for something big

  • Choose a goal (a phone, a bike, a trip)
  • How much does it cost? How long will you need to save?
  • Save a little money every week or month
  • It takes time, but you reach your goal
  • Saving feels hard, but it is worth it in the end
  • You can save faster with a small job or by spending less
  • What is YOUR saving goal? Talk about it!
Tip: put your saving money in a different place.
4
📊

Making a budget

  • A budget is a plan for your money
  • Example: you get 60 € a month
  • Split it: 50 % needs · 30 % wants · 20 % saving
  • That means: 30 € needs, 18 € wants, 12 € saving
  • Write down what you spend (notebook or app)
  • A budget helps you not to run out of money
  • How would you split your own money?
Tip: check your budget every week.
5
🛒

Smart shopping

  • Compare prices before you buy (shops and online)
  • Wait one day before you buy something big
  • Make a shopping list and stick to it
  • Don't buy something just because your friends have it
  • Check the quality — cheap is not always good
  • Watch out for tricks in adverts and shops
  • Tell about a good or bad buy you made
Tip: look for things on sale or a bargain.
6
💸

When your money runs out

  • Why money runs out too fast: too many small buys, no plan
  • Small things (drinks, snacks) add up quickly
  • Keep track of what you spend
  • Don't borrow money from friends — it can cause problems
  • Debt means you owe money — try to avoid it
  • What can you do when you have no money left?
  • Talk about a time your money ran out
Tip: save a little for the end of the month.
